Your rights

You can exercise these rights at any time, free of charge:

  • Access and portability (Art. 15, 20): download all your data as JSON from your account.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): change your display name on the account page; ask the contact above for anything else.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): delete your account, workflows, runs and files from the account page. Audit entries are kept for their retention period but detached from you.
  • Restriction and objection (Art. 18, 21), and withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)) through the Cookie settings link in the page footer.
  • Complaint to a supervisory authority (Art. 77){% if authority %}: {{ authority }}{% else %}, in particular in the EU country where you live or work{% endif %}.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes; sessions use HttpOnly, SameSite cookies; forms are protected against cross-site request forgery; each run's files are confined to its scratch directory; and the audit log records security-relevant events.
